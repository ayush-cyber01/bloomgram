# 🌸 Bloomgram

### Beautiful flowers, delivered digitally.

Bloomgram is a simple, elegant digital flower-gifting experience that lets you create and share virtual bouquets with someone you love.

Instead of sending physical flowers, Bloomgram lets you craft a personalized bouquet, add a message, and share the experience digitally — making a small gesture feel special from anywhere in the world.

🌐 **Live Demo:** https://bloomgram.vercel.app/

---

## ✨ Features

- 🌸 **Digital Bouquet Builder**
  - Create your own personalized flower bouquet.
  - Choose from different flowers and combine them into a unique arrangement.

- 💌 **Personal Messages**
  - Add a recipient and a custom message to your bouquet.
  - Turn a simple collection of flowers into a meaningful digital gift.

- 🌷 **Virtual Garden**
  - Explore a collection of example bouquets.
  - View flowers and messages created for different occasions.

- 🔗 **Shareable Bouquets**
  - Bouquets can be represented through URL parameters, making individual bouquet views easy to share.

- 📱 **Responsive Design**
  - Designed to work across desktop and mobile screens.

- ✨ **Elegant Animations**
  - Soft animations, floating petals, hover effects, and subtle transitions create a calm floral experience.

- 🎨 **Minimal Floral Design**
  - Uses a soft cream, blush, rose, and sage color palette.
  - Typography combines Cormorant Garamond and Jost for an elegant visual style.

---

## 🌷 How It Works

The experience is designed around three simple steps:

### 1. Choose Your Flowers

Open the bouquet builder and select the flowers you want to include.

### 2. Personalize Your Bouquet

Add:

- Recipient name
- Personal message
- Flower selection

### 3. Share the Bouquet

The bouquet can be opened through a dedicated view page, allowing it to be shared digitally with the recipient.

- HTML5
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript
- Google Fonts
